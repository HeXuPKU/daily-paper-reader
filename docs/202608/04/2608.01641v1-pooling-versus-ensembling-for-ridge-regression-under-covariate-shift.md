## 摘要
许多场景中的数据集自然划分为来自亚群体、批次效应或多个来源聚合的聚类。对这种异质性的常见响应是对每个聚类训练的学习器进行集成，而不是对合并数据拟合单一模型。先前激励此类方法的工作通常考虑聚类之间协变量分布和条件结果模型均不同的情形；而仅基于协变量分布的聚类感知划分与集成的作用仍有待探索。我们在线性结果模型下的岭正则化最小二乘回归中处理该情形，并考虑所有岭惩罚值$\lambda\geq 0$，包括$\lambda= 0$时无岭预测器的特殊情况。通过考虑固定效应和随机效应模型，我们论证在随机效应下，最优调参的池化岭预测器始终优于各自最优调参的预测器集成。对于固定效应，我们推导出池化与集成预测器的一般公式，以刻画回归系数和预测器分布偏移的作用。这些结果共同将先前关于使用岭和无岭回归预测器的袋装与随机划分估计的风险分析从i.i.d.设置推广到包含协变量偏移和异质性感知的划分结构。

## Abstract
Datasets in many settings naturally partition into clusters arising from sub-populations, batch effects, or aggregation across multiple sources. A common response to such heterogeneity is to ensemble learners trained on each cluster rather than fit a single model to the pooled data. Prior work motivating such approaches has typically considered settings in which both the covariate distribution and the conditional outcome model differ across clusters; the role of cluster-aware partitioning and ensembling based solely on the covariate distribution remains to be explored. We address this case for ridge-regularized least-squares regression under a linear outcome model and consider all ridge penalty values $λ\geq 0$, including the special case of the ridgeless predictor at $λ= 0$. By considering both fixed-effects and random-effects models, we argue that under random effects, an optimally tuned pooled ridge predictor always outperforms ensembles of individually optimally tuned predictors. For fixed effects, we derive a general formula for the pooled and ensembled predictors to characterize the role of both regression coefficients as well as the predictor distribution shifts. Together, these results generalize prior risk analyses of bagging and random-partition estimation using ridge and ridgeless regression predictors from the i.i.d. setting to encompass covariate shift and heterogeneity-aware partition structure.

## 摘要
本文研究了用大量辅助样本扩充微小目标样本的问题。利用Tukey分解，有两种常用方法：逆概率加权（IPW）和全似然（FL）方法。我们表明，IPW方法受限于目标样本量较小的问题，而FL方法可以以大量辅助样本的规模估计某些模型参数，我们将这种现象称为完全效率增益。我们研究了指数族和指数族混合模型下完全效率增益背后的理论。我们还研究了非参数程序下IPW方法的效率增益，并展示了它如何达到目标样本量的参数速率。作为附注，我们还讨论了如何使用FL同时训练目标分布和比值比模型的神经网络。

## Abstract
In this paper, we study the problem of augmenting a tiny target sample with a massive auxiliary sample. Utilizing Tukey's factorization, there are two popular approaches: the inverse probability weight (IPW) and the full-likelihood (FL) methods. We show that the IPW approach suffers from the limited target sample problem while the FL method may estimate some model parameters at the rate of the massive auxiliary sample size, a phenomenon we call full efficiency gain. We study the theory behind the full efficiency gain for exponential families and mixtures of exponential families. We also study the efficiency gain for the IPW method under a nonparametric procedure and show how it can achieve a parametric rate of the target sample size. As a side note, we also discuss how one may use FL to train neural network models simultaneously for both the target distribution and the odds model.

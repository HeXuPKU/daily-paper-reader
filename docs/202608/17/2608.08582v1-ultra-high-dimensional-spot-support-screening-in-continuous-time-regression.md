## 摘要
我们研究高频连续时间回归中的变量筛选，目标是点回归系数的支撑。我们在坐标截断和有限变差跳跃下建立了边际相关向量和协变量相关矩阵的局部估计的一致逐元素界。在局部平均α-Hölder条件下，速率为{((log p)/n)^(α/(2α+1))}，直到对数因子。这为边际支撑提供了确定筛选。我们还推导了一个列表大小的极小极大下界，当保留列表大小d满足log(p/d)≈log p时，该下界与局部化和多重性阶数匹配。对于局部依赖下的回归支撑恢复，我们提出了带路径Lepski自适应的偏残差点迭代筛选。在稀疏特征值和标准化beta-min下，该过程具有确定筛选性质，并且仅需要与当前活动集相关的相关列。模拟研究和高频因子应用说明了其性能。

## Abstract
We study variable screening for high-frequency continuous-time regression, targeting the support of the spot regression coefficient. We establish uniform entrywise bounds for local estimates of the marginal correlation vector and covariate correlation matrix under coordinatewise truncation and finite-variation jumps. Under a local averaged $α$-Hölder condition, the rate is $\{(\log p)/n\}^{α/(2α+1)}$, up to logarithmic factors. This yields sure screening for the marginal support. We also derive a list-size minimax lower bound that matches the localization and multiplicity order when the retained list size $d$ satisfies $\log(p/d)\asymp\log p$. For regression-support recovery under local dependence, we propose partial-residual spot iterative screening with pathwise Lepski adaptation. Under sparse eigenvalues and standardized beta-min, the procedure has the sure screening property and requires only correlation columns associated with the current active set. Simulation studies and a high-frequency factor application illustrate its performance.

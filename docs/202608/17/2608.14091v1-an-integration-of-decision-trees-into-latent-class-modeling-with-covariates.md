## 摘要
我们提出了一种将决策树拟合到潜在类别的新方法。潜在类别分析的方法学文献此前主要关注给定协变量下类别成员资格的Logistic模型，这在协变量之间存在复杂交互时具有重要缺陷：Logistic模型容易因省略某些交互项而被错误设定，且随着高阶交互项的加入，解释的复杂性迅速增加。我们提出的方法直接将决策树建模整合到潜在类别分析框架中，将协变量效应建模为易于解释的树，引导视线通过预测变量的组合到达最终的类别条件分类。这种新方法不需要对带有协变量的潜在类别模型进行任何非传统假设，基于成熟的模型估计程序，并且可以很容易地在现有软件中实现。在本文中，我们专注于二元协变量的决策树。我们介绍了所提出的方法，描述了两种树的剪枝策略，并提供了一个真实数据示例。重要的扩展包括通过开发更先进的预测变量内分裂程序将方法推广到多项和连续协变量，以及开发和评估替代的树剪枝策略。这项工作的最终目标是在潜在类别分析方法学文献中开辟一条新的研究路线，并通过一种新颖的数据分析工具促进应用研究的发展。

## Abstract
We propose a novel methodology for fitting decision trees to latent classes. The latent class analysis methodological literature has previously been focusing on logistic models of class membership given covariates, which has important drawbacks in the presence of complex interactions between covariates: logistic models are easily misspecified by omitting some interaction terms and complexity of interpretation increases rapidly with inclusion of higher-order interaction terms. Our proposed approach directly integrates decision tree modeling into the latent class analysis framework to model the covariate effects as an easily interpretable tree leading the eye through combinations of predictors to a final conditional classification. The novel methodology does not require any non-traditional assumptions for latent class models with covariates, is based on well-established routines of model estimation, and can be readily implemented in existing software. In the present paper, we focus on decision trees for binary covariates. We present the proposed approach, describe two tree pruning strategies, and provide a real-data illustration. Important extensions include generalizing the approach to multinomial and continuous covariates by developing more advanced within-predictor splitting procedures, and developing and evaluating alternative tree pruning strategies. The ultimate aim of this work is to initiate a novel research line in the latent class analysis methodological literature, and to facilitate the advancement of applied research via a novel data analytical tool.

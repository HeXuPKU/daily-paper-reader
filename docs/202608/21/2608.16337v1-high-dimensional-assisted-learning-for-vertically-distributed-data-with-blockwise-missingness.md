## 摘要
在多机构研究中，不同方持有部分重叠个体集合的不同特征块。某些记录的反应变量也可能缺失。在此类设置中，我们提出了无数据汇集或依赖协调服务器的分块缺失数据辅助学习（ALB），用于稀疏高维线性估计和坐标级推断。ALB通过循环块更新最小化正则化的可用案例二次损失。每个循环通过样本级线性汇总通信$O(n)$个标量，与数据维度$p$无关，并且迭代几何收敛到集中式解。我们推导了分离统计误差和优化误差的估计速率。对于目标系数的推断，ALB估计相应的精度列，并使用样本级方差估计器，该估计器考虑了重叠样本计算的矩之间的依赖性。在稀疏性和重叠条件下，学生化估计在$\sqrt n$速率下渐近标准正态，即使没有完整案例。我们还研究了一次性扰动协变量和响应释放，通过用噪声版本替换未扰动的样本级量来减少直接泄露。模拟和对多模式阿尔茨海默病神经影像学倡议数据的分析表明，ALB接近其集中式基准，并通过纳入部分观察记录改进了完整案例Lasso。

## Abstract
In multi-institutional studies, different parties hold distinct feature blocks for partially overlapping sets of individuals. Responses may also be missing for some records. In such settings, we propose Assisted Learning with Block-Missing Data (ALB) for sparse high-dimensional linear estimation and coordinatewise inference without pooling records or relying on a coordinating server. ALB minimizes a regularized available-case quadratic loss using cyclic block updates. Each cycle communicates $O(n)$ scalars through sample-level linear summaries, regardless of data dimension $p$, and the iterates converge geometrically to the centralized solution. We derive estimation rates that separate statistical and optimization errors. For inference on a target coefficient, ALB estimates the corresponding precision column and uses a sample-level variance estimator that accounts for dependence among moments computed from overlapping samples. Under sparsity and overlap conditions, the studentized estimator is asymptotically standard normal at the $\sqrt n$ rate, even when there are no complete cases. We also study one-time perturbed covariate and response releases that reduce direct disclosure by replacing unperturbed sample-level quantities with noisy versions. Simulations and an analysis of multimodal Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative data indicate that ALB approximates its centralized benchmark and improves upon complete-case Lasso by incorporating partially observed records.

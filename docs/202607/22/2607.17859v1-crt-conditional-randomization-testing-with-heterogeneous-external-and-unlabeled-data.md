## 摘要
条件随机化检验（CRT）为条件独立性检验提供了一种原则性方法，在真实条件分布已知时能保证精确的I类错误控制。然而实践中，该分布必须估计，估计误差会膨胀I类错误，而高维度和有限样本量可能降低检验效能。尽管外部和无标签数据为改进条件独立性检验提供了潜力，但忽视分布异质性的简单集成可能损害I类错误控制且无法增强检验效能。我们提出CRT*，一种稳健集成外部和无标签数据集以增强异质性场景下条件独立性检验的新框架。CRT*采用基于迁移学习的平滑残差自助法（SRB）进行条件分布估计，并通过检验统计量的最优凸组合实现自适应数据融合。我们从理论上证明，基于SRB的估计量在期望总变差距离下收敛到真实条件分布。此外，即使在高度正则化场景下，CRT*仍能维持有效的I类错误控制，并比不使用外部数据的标准CRT获得严格更高的检验效能。模拟实验和RNA-seq乳腺癌数据分析表明，在异质性设定下CRT*在维持I类错误控制的同时显著提升了检验效能。

## Abstract
The conditional randomization test (CRT) provides a principled approach to conditional independence (CI) testing, guaranteeing exact type-I error control when the true conditional distribution is known. In practice, however, this distribution must be estimated, and estimation errors can inflate type-I errors, while high dimensionality and limited sample sizes can reduce power. Although external and unlabeled data offer the potential to improve CI testing, naive integration that ignores distributional heterogeneity can compromise type-I error control and fail to enhance power. We propose \textbf{CRT*}, a novel framework that robustly integrates external and unlabeled datasets to enhance CI testing in heterogeneous scenarios. CRT* employs smooth residual-bootstrap (SRB) with transfer learning for conditional distribution estimation, combined with adaptive data fusion via an optimal convex combination of test statistics. We theoretically establish that the SRB-based estimator converges to the true conditional distribution in expected total variation distance. Furthermore, even in high-dimensional regimes, CRT* maintains valid type-I error control and achieves strictly higher power than standard CRT without external data. Simulations and RNA-seq breast cancer data analyses demonstrate that CRT* substantially improves power while maintaining type-I error control in heterogeneous settings.

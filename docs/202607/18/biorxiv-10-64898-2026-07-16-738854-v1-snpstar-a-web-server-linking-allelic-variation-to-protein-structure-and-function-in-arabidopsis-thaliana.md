## 摘要
理解自然遗传变异如何影响蛋白质结构和功能是植物生物学的核心问题。1001基因组计划在超过一千个拟南芥品系中编录了数百万个单核苷酸多态性（SNP），为将序列变异与三维蛋白质结构和种群背景联系起来提供了前所未有的机会。然而，实现这一目标需要能够在同一个可访问框架中整合这些尺度的工具。这里我们介绍SNPstar，一个通过以基因为中心的交互式界面，将拟南芥等位变异与AlphaFold3预测结构联系起来的网络服务器。SNPstar用描述性特征、热力学稳定性估计、蛋白质结构域背景以及全基因组关联结果注释每个变异，并计算将品系按共享DNA或蛋白质序列分组的单倍型和蛋白质型。研究人员可以在没有本地计算基础设施的情况下，表征变异、可视化其结构背景、绘制其地理分布图，并优先选择品系进行实验验证。我们通过两个案例研究演示了SNPstar。第一个案例重现了镉转运蛋白HMA3中已知的功能丧失变异，验证了SNPstar能够优先考虑功能相关的等位基因。第二个案例利用SNPstar定义的蛋白质型，在ARGONAUTE 2中识别出一个N端SNP组合，该组合区分了在体外siRNA指导的靶标切割中不同的品系，将蛋白质编码变异与可测量的分子表型联系起来。因此，SNPstar有助于将自然变异转化为机制性见解。

## Abstract
Understanding how natural genetic variants affect protein structure and function is central to plant biology. The 1001 Genomes Project has catalogued millions of single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) across more than a thousand Arabidopsis thaliana accessions, offering an unprecedented opportunity to relate sequence variation to three-dimensional protein structure and population context. Yet, realizing it requires tools that integrate these scales in one accessible framework. Here we present SNPstar, a web server that links allelic variation in A. thaliana to AlphaFold3-predicted structures through a gene-centric, interactive interface. SNPstar annotates each variant with descriptive features, thermodynamic stability estimates, protein domain context, and genome-wide association results, and computes haplotypes and proteotypes that group accessions by shared DNA or protein sequence. Researchers can characterize variants, visualize their structural context, map their geographic distribution, and prioritize accessions for experimental validation without local computational infrastructure. We demonstrate SNPstar with two case studies. The first recapitulates known loss-of-function variation in the cadmium transporter HMA3, validating that SNPstar prioritizes functionally consequential alleles. The second uses SNPstar-defined proteotypes to identify an N-terminal SNP combination in ARGONAUTE 2 that distinguishes accessions differing in in vitro siRNA-directed target cleavage, linking protein-coding variation to a measurable molecular phenotype. SNPstar thus helps translate natural variation into mechanistic insight.

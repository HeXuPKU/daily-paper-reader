## 摘要
许多基因的功能仍未知晓，而依赖基因突变频率的传统驱动基因发现方法无法评估那些仅罕见突变的基因。本研究将基于Evo~2的基因组分析与常规临床影像相结合，在全基因组规模上识别基因-表型关联。针对三个TCGA队列（cRCC=透明细胞肾细胞癌、HCC=肝细胞癌、BC=乳腺癌；总计n=340）中的每个体细胞突变，Evo~2在无任务特定训练的情况下预测了一个严重性评分。随后，每个基因的严重性汇总与从配对肿瘤分割中提取的影像组学特征进行相关性分析，并控制总突变负荷。在TCGA-cRCC（n=162）中，这一扫描恢复了已知的肾癌驱动基因，并额外识别出46个达到错误发现率（FDR）显著性的基因，这些基因不在精选的癌症基因面板中，其中几个是孟德尔纤毛病和细胞骨架疾病基因。这些结果表明，将基因组语言模型与广泛可用的临床影像配对，可作为一种无假设的发现工具，揭示传统方法无法识别的基因-影像关联。

## Abstract
The function of many genes is still unknown, and conventional driver-discovery methods, which rely on how frequently a gene is mutated, cannot assess genes that are only rarely affected. Here we pair Evo~2-based genome analysis with routine clinical imaging to identify gene--phenotype associations at genome-wide scale. For every somatic mutation across three TCGA cohorts (cRCC=clear cell renal cell carcinoma, HCC=hepatocellular carcinoma, and BC=breast cancer; $n = 340$ total), Evo~2 predicts a severity score, with no task-specific training. Per-gene severity summaries are then correlated with radiomic features extracted from paired tumor segmentations, controlling for total mutation burden. In TCGA-cRCC ($n = 162$), this sweep recovers established renal-cancer drivers and identifies 46 additional genes reaching false discovery rate (FDR) significance absent from curated cancer-gene panels, several of which are Mendelian ciliopathy and cytoskeletal-disease genes. These results demonstrate that pairing a genomic language model with widely available clinical imaging can serve as a hypothesis-free discovery tool for gene--imaging associations invisible to conventional approaches.

## 摘要
稀疏回归既需要估计，也需要决定保留哪些效应。贝叶斯收缩为这一决策提供不确定性，但更丰富的先验层级可能使校准和后验计算变得困难。我们提出一个计算高效的经验贝叶斯框架，用于基于凸惩罚和对数凹先验的高斯稀疏回归。观测尺度和全局收缩参数分别校准，众数概括联合后验信息并提供系数估计，近端采样器提供后验不确定性。然后，一个后验尺度上的幅度阈值和激活概率将这些输出转化为稀疏决策。同一近端结构贯穿始终地被重复使用，使优化和采样成本低廉，并允许扩展到具有可处理近端映射的其他凸惩罚。我们还发展了仿射信息下的经验贝叶斯校准，区分硬齐次约束与软非齐次仿射信息，并在强仿射信息造成低秩刚性时引入几何感知的后验预条件。合成实验表明：当样本量超过预测变量数时能够准确恢复，当预测变量数超过观测数时表现出保守的弱信号行为，并且几何感知缩放显著提升蒙特卡洛效率。在一个糖尿病数据集上，后验不确定性与已有贝叶斯分析密切一致，而最终决策给出了更稀疏的实用总结。

## Abstract
Sparse regression requires both estimation and a decision about which effects to retain. Bayesian shrinkage supplies uncertainty for that decision, but richer prior hierarchies can make calibration and posterior computation demanding. We develop a computationally efficient empirical Bayes framework for Gaussian sparse regression based on convex penalties and log-concave priors. The observation scale and global shrinkage parameter are calibrated separately, the mode summarises information from the joint posterior and provides coefficient estimates, and a proximal sampler supplies posterior uncertainty. A posterior-scale magnitude threshold and activation probability then convert these outputs into a sparse decision. The same proximal structure is reused throughout, making optimisation and sampling inexpensive and allowing extensions to other convex penalties with tractable proximal maps. We also develop empirical Bayes calibration under affine information, distinguishing hard homogeneous constraints from soft nonhomogeneous affine information, and introduce geometry-aware posterior preconditioning when strong affine information creates low-rank stiffness. Synthetic experiments show accurate recovery when the sample size exceeds the number of predictors, conservative weak-signal behaviour when predictors outnumber observations, and substantial gains in Monte Carlo efficiency from geometry-aware scaling. On a diabetes dataset, posterior uncertainty agrees closely with established Bayesian analyses while the terminal decision provides a sparser practical summary.

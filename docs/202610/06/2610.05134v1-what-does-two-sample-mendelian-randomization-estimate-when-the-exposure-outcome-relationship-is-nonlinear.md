## 摘要
利用汇总统计量的两样本孟德尔随机化已成为流行病学中一种流行的MR设计。其比值估计只有在包括线性性在内的强且不可检验的假设下，才具有平均因果效应的解释。我们推导了当连续暴露具有非线性效应时，在关于遗传变异和未测量因素如何塑造暴露的两个条件下：不存在未测量的共同效应修饰因子，以及存在暴露控制函数，每个变异的比值以及由其构建的合并估计量和稳健估计量估计的是什么。在任一条件下，每个变异的比值都是剂量-反应曲线斜率的加权平均，其权重取决于该变异如何改变暴露分布，并且当不同基因型下的暴露分布不相交时，这些权重为非负。使所有人的暴露同等程度地发生改变的变异，所针对的几乎是同一个量；在特定剂量处起作用或作用于暴露离散程度的变异，所针对的则是不同的量，其中一些落在任何个体效应范围之外。因此，合并估计量没有固定的解释，异质性检验和MR-Egger会将非线性误读为多效性，而且即使剂量-反应曲线可由个体水平数据识别，汇总统计量也无法揭示该问题。对于连续暴露，两样本估计依赖于线性性假设，该假设应被明确陈述并加以辩护；个体水平暴露数据可解决该问题，应被优先考虑。

## Abstract
Two-sample Mendelian randomization with summary statistics has become a popular MR design in Epidemiology. Its ratio estimate has an average causal effect interpretation only under strong untestable assumptions including linearity. We derive what the per-variant ratio, and the pooled and robust estimators built from it, estimate when a continuous exposure has a nonlinear effect, under two conditions on how genetic variation and unmeasured factors shape exposure: no unmeasured common effect modifier, and an exposure control function. Under either condition, each variant's ratio is a weighted average of the slope of the dose-response curve, with weights that depend on how that variant moves the distribution of exposure and that are nonnegative when the exposure distributions at different genotypes do not cross. Variants that shift everyone's exposure equally target nearly the same quantity; variants acting at particular doses or on the spread of exposure target different ones, some outside the range of any individual effect. Pooled estimates therefore have no fixed interpretation, heterogeneity tests and MR-Egger misread nonlinearity as pleiotropy, and summary statistics cannot reveal the problem, even when the dose-response curve is identifiable from individual-level data. For continuous exposures, two-sample estimates rest on a linearity assumption that should be stated and defended; individual-level exposure data resolve the problem and should be prioritized.

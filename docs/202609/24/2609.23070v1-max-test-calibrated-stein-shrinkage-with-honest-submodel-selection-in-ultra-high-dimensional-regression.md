## 摘要
古典预检验和 Stein 型估计量在受限回归拟合与全回归拟合之间进行插值，但当 p≫n 且约束为数据自适应时，OLS 和卡方校准会失效。我们提出一个诚实样本分离框架，围绕一个共同的选择零分布律构建。独立的选择数据扩展一个强制核心；单独的估计数据将一个完整设计的正则化全模型（FM）拟合与一个全新的精确零子模型重拟合配对。一个最大残差关联统计量评估所有被排除的坐标。投影高斯抽样在同方差高斯误差下再现其条件零分布，从而产生有限样本有效的秩检验。同一设计特定分布的逆矩替代 q−2，并校准预检验（PT）、Stein 型（S）和正部 Stein 型（PS）规则。我们推导精确的端点风险公式、诚实条件有效性、零分布律集中性、逆矩一致性、显式选择失败余项，以及在无一致占优声明下的端点自适应性。包含 2,000 次重复且预测变量多达 30,000 个的高斯实验，在共同的子模型、检验和校准下，比较了通过冻结折交叉验证调优的 Ridge 和 LASSO FM。PS 在近零系数风险方面提供大幅增益，并在强偏离下接近相应的全模型风险。一个单独的 CPSS-LASSO/CPSS-MCP 审计评估数据自适应选择，而一项包含 19,152 个预测变量的分样本 DepMap 研究说明了预测增益以及对误差假设的敏感性。HDMaxShrink R 包实现了该过程。

## Abstract
Classical preliminary-test and Stein-type estimators interpolate between restricted and full regression fits, but OLS and chi-squared calibration fail when $p\gg n$ and the restriction is data-adaptive. We propose an honest sample-separated framework built around a common selected-null law. Independent selection data extend a mandatory core; separate estimation data pair a complete-design regularized full-model (FM) fit with a fresh exact-null submodel refit. A maximum residual-association statistic assesses all excluded coordinates. Projected Gaussian draws reproduce its conditional null distribution under homoskedastic Gaussian errors, yielding a finite-sample-valid rank test. The inverse moment of the same design-specific law replaces $q-2$ and calibrates preliminary-test (PT), Stein-type (S), and positive-part Stein-type (PS) rules. We derive exact endpoint-risk formulas, honest conditional validity, null-law concentration, inverse-moment consistency, an explicit selection-failure remainder, and endpoint adaptivity without uniform-dominance claims. Gaussian experiments with 2,000 replications and up to 30,000 predictors compare Ridge and LASSO FMs, tuned by frozen-fold cross-validation, under a common submodel, test, and calibration. PS provides large near-null coefficient-risk gains and approaches the relevant full-model risk under strong departures. A separate CPSS-LASSO/CPSS-MCP audit evaluates data-adaptive selection, while a split-sample DepMap study with 19,152 predictors illustrates prediction gains and sensitivity to error assumptions. The HDMaxShrink R package implements the procedure.
